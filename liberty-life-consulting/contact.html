Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I hear back from you?
I aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please indicate this in your message subject line.
Do you offer free consultations?
Yes! I offer a complimentary 15-minute discovery call to discuss your goals and see if we're a good fit to work together. This helps ensure my coaching approach aligns with your needs.
Are sessions available online?
Absolutely! I offer both in-person and virtual coaching sessions via secure video platforms. Many clients find virtual sessions just as effective and appreciate the convenience.
What should I expect from our first session?
Our first session focuses on understanding your story, current challenges, and goals. We'll explore where you are now, where you want to be, and begin creating a personalized roadmap for your transformation journey.

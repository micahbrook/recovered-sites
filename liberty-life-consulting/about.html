About Liberty Life Consulting

Empowering women to rise, embrace their strength, and live their fullest life

Building a Better Tomorrow, Today

Liberty Life Consulting was born from a deep understanding that every woman deserves to live free from the limitations of past trauma and limiting beliefs. We believe in the transformative power of community, faith, and resilience.

Our mission is to empower individuals from diverse backgrounds to rise above adversity and step into their fullest potential. Through personalized coaching, transformative retreats, and empowering workshops, we create safe spaces where healing happens and dreams take flight.

We're here to guide you on your journey from survival to thriving, from wounded to warrior, from limited to liberated. Your story of resilience starts here.

Kylie Smith

Founder & Lead Life Coach | President of Rising Resilient Australia Inc.

With over 20 years of experience as an educator, keynote speaker, author, and trauma recovery specialist, Kylie brings a unique blend of professional expertise and personal understanding to her work. As the founder of Liberty Life Consulting and President of Rising Resilient Australia Inc., she has dedicated her life to helping women overcome trauma, embrace their strength, and step into their purpose.

Kylie's warm, faith-infused approach creates a safe space where transformation happens naturally. Her authentic vulnerability and unwavering belief in the resilience of the human spirit inspire women worldwide to write new chapters in their stories.
